Privacy Policy

How Petros Curious Mind handles information when you visit or use the website.

Last updated: 26 November 2026

1. About this policy

This policy explains what information may be collected when you visit Petros Curious Mind, create an account, comment on a story or contact us. It also explains browser storage and how advertising-related technologies may be used if advertising is introduced. Please read it together with our Cookie and Browser Storage Policy.

2. Information you provide

Depending on how you use the website, information you choose to provide may include:

  • Your name or display name and email address when you register or manage an account.
  • Information in your profile and account preferences.
  • Comments, reactions, messages and other content you submit.
  • Information included in a message sent through the contact form.
  • Story submissions or other material you voluntarily send to us.

Please do not submit passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information in public comments or messages.

3. Information collected through use

Our hosting, security and application services may process technical information needed to deliver and protect the website. This can include IP address, browser and device information, request and error logs, approximate usage information and timestamps. The exact information and retention period depend on the relevant service provider and its settings.

4. How we use information

  • To create, authenticate and manage accounts.
  • To display profiles, comments and story-related interactions you choose to make.
  • To receive, review and respond to contact messages.
  • To operate, maintain, troubleshoot and secure the website.
  • To prevent spam, abuse, fraud and violations of our Terms.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

5. Service providers and sharing

We use third-party providers to operate parts of the website. These currently include hosting and delivery services, the Supabase platform for application data and account services, Resend for email delivery, and Blogger-hosted image delivery for some story images. Those providers process information as needed to provide their services and are subject to their own terms and privacy practices. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information where required by law, to protect the website or its users, or as part of a legitimate business or service transfer.

6. Cookies, local storage and similar technologies

The website uses browser storage for functions such as keeping your sign-in session and remembering appearance preferences where enabled. These technologies may include cookies or local storage. They help the website operate and remember choices. Details are provided in our Cookie and Browser Storage Policy.

Third-party advertising is not represented as currently active on this website. If Google AdSense or another advertising service is introduced, that service and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar identifiers to deliver, measure or personalise advertisements, subject to applicable consent requirements. Before advertising is enabled, this policy and the cookie controls will be updated to identify the services actually in use and explain available choices.

When Google advertising is used, Google may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Users can review or change Google's advertising settings at Google Ads Settings. More information about how Google uses information is available in Google's information about partner sites.

7. Comments and public content

Comments submitted to stories are subject to review before they appear publicly. If approved, the comment and the display name associated with it may be visible to other visitors. Do not include private contact details or information you do not want made public.

8. Data security and retention

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, manage accounts and content, resolve disputes, enforce our terms or meet legal obligations. Retention periods can vary by data type and provider.

9. Your choices and account deletion

You can manage certain profile details and browser preferences through the website. You may request permanent account deletion using the account controls available on your profile page. Account deletion removes the authentication account, profile and associated account activity as described by the deletion process. Stories you authored may remain available to readers without being linked to your account. Technical backups may retain data temporarily under the provider's backup-retention process.

You can also manage optional browser preferences using the cookie settings control on this website. Browser settings may allow you to clear or block cookies and local storage, although doing so may affect sign-in and saved preferences.

10. Children

This website is intended for a general audience and is not knowingly designed to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the matter.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website or its services change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated date.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please use our Contact page.
