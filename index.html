Stories connect us

Every story has a story behind it.

Folklore carries the memories, beliefs, creativity, and wisdom of people. It lives in the stories shared around homes, passed down through generations, and retold in new ways. At Petros Curious Mind, we bring these stories together in one place for curious minds to explore.

Our purpose

Keeping stories alive

Petros Curious Mind exists to celebrate folklore and the power of storytelling. We want to make it easy for readers to discover stories, appreciate the cultures and ideas behind them, and keep the tradition of sharing stories alive.
