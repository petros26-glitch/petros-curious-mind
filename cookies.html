Cookie and Browser Storage Policy

How this website uses browser storage and how you can manage your choices.

Last updated: 26 November 2026

1. What are cookies and browser storage?

Cookies are small files stored by a website in your browser. Websites can also use local storage and similar technologies to remember information between visits. This policy uses “browser storage” to refer to these technologies together.

2. What this website currently uses

  • Sign-in and security: the authentication service may use browser storage and related mechanisms to maintain your account session and protect account features.
  • Appearance preferences: the site can remember the light or dark appearance and colour theme you choose.
  • Consent choice: this site stores your cookie preference choice so it can remember whether you accepted or rejected optional categories.

3. Analytics and advertising

At the time this policy was updated, this website's source does not intentionally load Google Analytics or Google AdSense advertising code. The consent controls include categories for future integrations, but selecting a category does not itself activate an analytics or advertising service.

If Google AdSense is introduced, Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses and similar identifiers to serve, measure and personalise ads. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this website or other websites. You can manage Google's advertising personalisation through Google Ads Settings. Learn more in Google's explanation of how it uses information on partner sites.

Any future analytics or advertising provider will be identified in this policy when it is actually integrated. Third-party providers may have their own privacy and cookie policies.

4. Your choices

Use the cookie settings button shown on this website to review or change optional preference categories. Necessary storage for core functions such as security and sign-in cannot be switched off through this control. You can also clear or block browser storage through your browser settings, but some site features may then stop working or forget your choices.

5. Visitors in the EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland

Where required, advertising services must obtain valid consent and use an appropriate consent solution. Google requires publishers serving personalised ads in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland to use a Google-certified consent management platform integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. This website's simple preference panel is not represented as a Google-certified CMP or as an IAB TCF integration. Before serving ads in regions where such a solution is required, the appropriate certified CMP and consent configuration must be implemented.

6. Updates

This policy may change when browser technologies or third-party services are added or removed. The current version will be published on this page.

7. Contact

For questions about browser storage or privacy, use our Contact page. See also our Privacy Policy.
