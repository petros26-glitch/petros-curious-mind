Who we are

A place for stories worth remembering.

Petros Curious Mind is a storytelling platform dedicated to folklore and the stories shared across generations. We believe stories carry more than words: they hold history, imagination, wisdom, culture and lessons that connect people.

01

Our purpose

To create a welcoming place where curious minds can discover, explore and enjoy folklore from different backgrounds and traditions, while keeping the value of traditional storytelling alive.

02

Our vision

We envision a world where stories continue to inspire, educate and connect people, and where the beauty of folklore is appreciated by generations to come.

03

How it began

Petros Curious Mind grew from a curiosity about the stories behind cultures, traditions and the world around us. Our journey is about keeping the spirit of storytelling alive, one story at a time.
