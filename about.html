Who we are

A place for stories worth remembering.

Petros Curious Mind is an independent storytelling platform focused on folklore and the stories people pass from one generation to another. We believe stories can preserve memory, invite curiosity and help readers explore the values, fears, hopes and imagination found in different communities.

01

Our purpose

We make space for readers to discover and enjoy stories, including original creative works and thoughtful retellings. Our aim is to celebrate storytelling while being clear about the difference between a traditional account, a retelling and an original fictional story.

02

Our editorial approach

Stories are prepared for readability and presented with titles, descriptions and relevant context. When a story is based on an identifiable tradition or source, we aim to name that source or acknowledge the tradition where reliable information is available. When a story is invented or substantially imagined, we aim to describe it as fiction or a creative interpretation rather than present it as proven history or science.

03

Respect for stories and their origins

Folklore belongs to living communities and is often shared in many versions. We recognise that a single retelling cannot represent every version of a tradition. We welcome thoughtful corrections and relevant source information from readers.

04

Why Petros Curious Mind exists

The platform was created to give stories a home and encourage readers to look beyond the surface of a tale. Its guiding idea is simple: there is often a history, a question, a lesson or an imaginative world behind the stories we remember.
