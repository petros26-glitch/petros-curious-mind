About Petros Curious Mind

The Story Behind Every Story.

About Us

Petros Curious Mind is a storytelling platform dedicated to folklore and the stories that have been shared across generations.

We believe stories are more than words. They carry history, imagination, wisdom, culture and lessons that connect people across different generations.

Our Purpose

Our purpose is to create a place where readers can discover, explore and enjoy folklore from different backgrounds and traditions.

We want to make storytelling accessible to curious minds and help preserve the value of traditional stories.

Our Vision

We envision a world where stories continue to inspire, educate and connect people.

Through Petros Curious Mind, we hope to build a growing storytelling community that appreciates the beauty and importance of folklore.

Our Story

Every story has a beginning, and every tradition has something worth discovering.

Petros Curious Mind was created from a curiosity about the stories behind cultures, traditions and the world around us.

Our journey is about keeping the spirit of storytelling alive, one story at a time.
